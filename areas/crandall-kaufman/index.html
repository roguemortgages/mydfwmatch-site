Moving to Crandall, Kaufman & Heartland

Newer master-planned communities southeast of Dallas, plus the county-seat town of Kaufman.

The tradeoff: Long commutes; special district taxes are common in new communities.

Kaufman County

How the drive compares

  • Southern Dallas County / Inland Port: Moderate
  • Downtown / Uptown Dallas: Moderate
  • Garland / Mesquite industrial corridor: Moderate
  • Waxahachie / Midlothian: Longer
  • Greenville: Longer
  • Richardson Telecom Corridor / CityLine: Longer
  • Las Colinas / Irving: Longest
  • Plano–Frisco corridor (Legacy West): Longest
  • DFW Airport: Longest
  • Westlake / Southlake corporate campuses: Longest
  • AllianceTexas / North Fort Worth: Longest
  • Downtown Fort Worth: Longest
  • Denton: Longest

Relative comparison only. Check your actual address at weekday rush hour before you commit.

Public schools

School district boundaries don't follow city lines. Confirm the district for any specific address, and review official ratings at the Texas Education Agency's TXschools.gov.

What owning here really costs

Your payment depends on the combined tax rate for the exact property (city, county, school district, and any MUD or PID), plus insurance and HOA dues. Two homes at the same price can have very different payments.
