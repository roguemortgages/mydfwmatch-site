59 places to start looking in DFW.

From high-rises in Uptown to acreage out in Ellis and Kaufman counties. Each guide shows the rush-hour drive to every major job center and what to check before you buy. Facts only. You decide what fits.

Dallas County

Downtown, Uptown & Deep EllumHigh-rises, mid-rises and townhomes in the most walkable part of the metro. Groceries, coffee and dinner are usually a short walk, and downtown jobs are minutes away.East Dallas (Lakewood, M Streets, White Rock)Lakewood, the M Streets and the neighborhoods around White Rock Lake. Older homes with real character, local shops and restaurants close by, and the lake a few minutes away.Oak Cliff (Bishop Arts, Kessler, Kidd Springs)Bishop Arts, Kessler and Kidd Springs, just south of downtown. Historic homes, lots of local restaurants and shops, and about a 12-minute drive into downtown Dallas.Highland Park & University ParkHighland Park and University Park. Close-in, established neighborhoods with walkable shopping villages and a mix of original homes and rebuilds.North Dallas (Preston Hollow to Far North Dallas)Preston Hollow up through Far North Dallas. A big area with lots of shopping and dining nearby, about 18 minutes from both downtown and Legacy at rush hour.Lake HighlandsMid-century and established homes with mature trees just northeast of White Rock Lake.RichardsonEstablished neighborhoods, DART rail and the Telecom Corridor right in town. If you work at CityLine or the Telecom Corridor, the drive is about eight minutes.GarlandEstablished neighborhoods, a historic downtown square and Lake Ray Hubbard on the east side.Rowlett & SachseSuburban neighborhoods along Lake Ray Hubbard, with most homes built from the 1980s on.MesquiteEstablished suburban neighborhoods east of Dallas, with shopping along I-635 and quick access to the Garland and Mesquite job corridor.Irving & Las ColinasCentral to almost everything: Las Colinas jobs, DFW Airport and both downtowns are within reach. Las Colinas itself has urban-style living around Lake Carolyn.CoppellEstablished suburban neighborhoods tucked between DFW Airport and Las Colinas. Most homes were built in the '80s through the 2000s, and the airport is about 15 minutes away.Carrollton & Farmers BranchEstablished neighborhoods with DART rail and a historic downtown square in Carrollton, about 17 minutes from both Las Colinas and Legacy.Grand PrairieRight between Dallas and Fort Worth, with newer development on the south end near Joe Pool Lake.Cedar Hill, DeSoto & DuncanvilleCedar Hill, DeSoto and Duncanville. Some of the only real hills in DFW, Cedar Hill State Park on Joe Pool Lake, and mostly established homes with room to breathe.Lancaster & Glenn HeightsEstablished and newer neighborhoods, with Lancaster's historic town square and the southern Dallas County logistics hub about 12 minutes away.Southeast Dallas County (Pleasant Grove, Balch Springs, Seagoville)Pleasant Grove, Balch Springs and Seagoville. Established neighborhoods with more room than you'd expect this close to downtown Dallas.

Collin County

AllenSuburban neighborhoods, most built from the '80s on, with lots of shopping close by and the Telecom Corridor about 18 minutes away.Prosper & CelinaNewer master-planned communities, many with bigger lots. About 63% of homes here were built in 2010 or later.Wylie & MurphySuburban neighborhoods near Lake Lavon, with a historic downtown in Wylie and the Telecom Corridor about 17 minutes away.Melissa & AnnaNewer communities on the northern growth edge along US-75. Most homes here were built in 2010 or later.Princeton & FarmersvilleFast-growing newer subdivisions east of McKinney, plus small-town Farmersville.Lucas, Fairview & ParkerAcreage and larger-lot living near Lake Lavon, with the Village at Fairview for shopping.West PlanoEstablished neighborhoods next to Legacy West and the Shops at Legacy. Most homes were built in the '80s through the 2000s, and Legacy is about 14 minutes away.East PlanoEstablished neighborhoods, historic Downtown Plano and DART rail, with the Telecom Corridor about 10 minutes away.West FriscoNewer master-planned neighborhoods near Lewisville Lake, with Legacy about 12 minutes away.East FriscoCentral Frisco, with The Star, downtown Frisco and major shopping and entertainment close by.West McKinneyNewer neighborhoods and large planned communities west of US-75.East McKinney & Historic DowntownMcKinney's historic downtown square, the older neighborhoods around it and new growth to the east.

Tarrant County

GrapevineHistoric Main Street, Grapevine Lake and the shortest drive to DFW Airport of any area here, about 12 minutes.Southlake, Colleyville & KellerLarger-lot suburbs in northeast Tarrant County, with Southlake Town Square and the Westlake corporate campuses about 16 minutes away.Hurst, Euless, Bedford & North Richland HillsHurst, Euless, Bedford and North Richland Hills. Established neighborhoods right between Dallas and Fort Worth, with TEXRail stations in North Richland Hills.Central Fort Worth (Near Southside, Cultural District, Fairmount, TCU)Near Southside, the Cultural District, Fairmount and the TCU area. Historic neighborhoods, Fort Worth's most walkable streets and downtown about nine minutes away.North Fort Worth & SaginawLarge newer subdivisions near AllianceTexas and I-35W, with lots of homes built in the last 15 years.West Fort Worth & White SettlementWest of Loop 820 around White Settlement, near Naval Air Station Fort Worth, Lake Worth and the I-30 corridor. Downtown Fort Worth is about 17 minutes away.Benbrook & Southwest Fort WorthEstablished neighborhoods near Benbrook Lake, about 15 minutes from downtown Fort Worth.MansfieldSuburban neighborhoods, a historic downtown and newer subdivisions near Joe Pool Lake.AzleA small city northwest of Fort Worth on Eagle Mountain Lake, with larger lots and a more rural feel.Haltom City, Watauga & Richland HillsEstablished neighborhoods about 15 minutes from downtown Fort Worth.North ArlingtonEstablished neighborhoods near the Entertainment District, UT Arlington and I-30, about 25 minutes from both DFW Airport and downtown Fort Worth.South ArlingtonSuburban neighborhoods, most built in the '80s through the 2000s, near Joe Pool Lake and the Arlington Highlands.

Denton County

DentonCollege-town energy, a walkable historic square and housing from every era. If you work in Denton, the commute basically disappears.Lewisville & Highland VillageCentral Denton County next to Lewisville Lake, with A-train rail and both Legacy and Denton about 20 minutes away.Flower Mound & ArgyleBigger lots and a more open feel near Grapevine Lake, with most homes built from the '80s on.Little Elm & The ColonyLakeside living on Lewisville Lake (most homes are less than a mile from the shore), and Legacy is about 18 minutes away.Northlake, Justin & HasletNew master-planned communities near AllianceTexas, which is about 18 minutes away. Most homes are 2010 or newer.Aubrey, Cross Roads & Oak PointNewer communities and open land northeast of Denton, close to Lewisville Lake.Sanger, Krum & Pilot PointSmall towns and open land north of Denton near Lake Ray Roberts.Corinth, Lake Dallas & Hickory CreekSuburban neighborhoods along I-35E next to Lewisville Lake, about 15 minutes from Denton.

Kaufman County

Forney & SunnyvaleLarge newer communities east of Dallas along US-80, many of them master-planned.TerrellA small city east of Dallas with an established downtown and open land around it.Crandall, Kaufman & HeartlandNewer master-planned communities southeast of Dallas, plus the county-seat town of Kaufman.

Ellis County

Midlothian & WaxahachieMidlothian and Waxahachie. Larger lots, acreage options and the historic courthouse square in Waxahachie.Red Oak & OvillaGrowing communities with larger lots just south of Dallas County.EnnisA historic small city on I-45, with Bardwell Lake close by and newer neighborhoods on its edges.

Rockwall County

Rockwall & HeathLake Ray Hubbard shoreline, a historic downtown square and newer neighborhoods.Royse City & FateNewer communities on the eastern growth edge. More than half the homes were built in 2010 or later.

Wise County

Decatur, Rhome & Wise CountyAcreage, ranch land and a historic courthouse square northwest of Fort Worth.

Hunt County

Greenville & Caddo MillsSmall-city living with a historic downtown and room to spread out on the far east side.
