[ Fair housing commitment ]

Your preferences belong in the result. Protected characteristics do not.

myDFWmatch is committed to equal housing opportunity. The Fair Housing Act prohibits discrimination in housing-related activities because of race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status or disability. State and local law may protect additional characteristics.

What shapes a match

The match compares broad search areas using your commute, outdoor space, home age, everyday errands, walkability, lake access, rural or planned-community preference and the other choices shown directly in the quiz. The method page explains the scoring and the source data.

What does not shape a match

The quiz never asks about, and the model never scores, race, color, national origin, religion, sex, familial status, disability or any other protected characteristic. It does not rate residents, schools, crime, safety or whether a neighborhood is “good.”

The decision stays yours

A match is a starting comparison, not an instruction about where you should or shouldn't live. You can explore any community. Check the facts that matter to you using neutral, reliable sources and property-specific information. For schools, that means the official data at TXschools.gov.

Questions or concerns

If anything on this site reads otherwise, tell me and I'll fix it. You can also learn about federal fair housing rights or report possible discrimination through the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development.
