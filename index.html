The part most relocation guides skip

Same price. Very different payment.

Texas doesn't have a state income tax, so property taxes do a lot of the heavy lifting. The rate depends on the city, the county, the school district and sometimes which side of the street you're on (hello, MUDs and PIDs). Two homes with the same price can come with very different monthly payments.

So before you fall in love with a ZIP code, know what owning there actually costs.
