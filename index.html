Texas property taxes

Two homes at the same price can have very different payments.

Texas doesn't have a state income tax, so property taxes do a lot of the heavy lifting. The rate depends on the city, the county, the school district and sometimes which side of the street you're on (and in a lot of newer areas, a MUD or PID). Two homes with the same price can come with very different monthly payments.

So before you fall in love with a ZIP code, know what owning there actually costs.
