[ About ]

Born in Dallas. Moved…a lot. Back in Texas.

Hi, I'm Julie. I built myDFWmatch because “Where should we live?” is a heck of a lot harder to answer when you don't know the map yet.

I was born and raised in Dallas. Then came Montana, a decade in Colorado and eleven years in Washington, where I became an Army spouse and a mortgage advisor. Now I'm back in the slow, country life of small-town Texas.

So I know the feeling. A new job, a move date that keeps creeping closer and approximately 47 browser tabs open about which side of town makes sense. Everybody has an opinion. Your cousin. TikTok. The coworker who moved to Frisco in 2019 and is suddenly a real estate economist.

myDFWmatch skips the opinions. It takes YOUR answers about the commute, the house and the everyday stuff, and runs them against real rush-hour drive times, Census housing data and actual maps of what's nearby. Then it tells you where I'd start looking, why, and what you'd be giving up.

The day job

I'm a Senior Mortgage Advisor & Strategist with Fairway Home Mortgage, and Rogue Mortgages is my brand. I'm the person you call when the mortgage question isn't really just a mortgage question. Relocating is one of those. The place and the payment are tangled together, especially in Texas, where property taxes can move a payment as much as the price does.

I'm going to ask questions. A lot of them. Not to make it complicated, but because the details change the answer.

What this is (and isn't)

A useful starting point.

Broad search areas, built around how you live, with the tradeoffs left right where you can see them.

Not a “best neighborhoods” list.

It doesn't rate schools, crime, safety or the people who live anywhere. It describes geography, housing and amenities. You decide what fits.

Not a crystal ball.

The exact address, today's listings and your real commute still get the final word. Here's how the match works.
